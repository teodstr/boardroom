# Roadmap

Frameworks we'd love to add. Each one is a good first contribution: pick one, open an issue saying you're on it, and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Strategy

- [ ] `mckinsey-7s`: organizational alignment (Waterman, Peters & Phillips, 1980)
- [ ] `value-chain`: primary and support activities (Porter, 1985)
- [ ] `generic-strategies`: cost leadership, differentiation, focus (Porter, 1980)
- [ ] `ge-mckinsey-matrix`: industry attractiveness vs business strength
- [ ] `scenario-planning`: plausible futures and robust strategies
- [ ] `balanced-scorecard`: strategy turned into objectives and indicators (Kaplan & Norton, 1992)
- [ ] `disruptive-innovation`: how entrants overturn incumbents (Christensen, 1997)

## Marketing

- [ ] `customer-journey`: touchpoints, emotions and pain points
- [ ] `jobs-to-be-done`: what customers "hire" a product to do
- [ ] `pricing-research`: Van Westendorp price sensitivity meter
- [ ] `brand-equity`: Keller's customer-based brand equity pyramid
- [ ] `aarrr-funnel`: acquisition, activation, retention, referral, revenue
- [ ] `net-promoter-score`: measuring loyalty and acting on it

## Finance

- [ ] `financial-ratios`: profitability, liquidity, solvency, efficiency
- [ ] `dcf-valuation`: discounted cash flow valuation
- [ ] `investment-appraisal`: NPV, IRR, payback

## Organization and people

- [ ] `kotter-change`: the eight steps of leading change (Kotter, 1996)
- [ ] `stakeholder-mapping`: power–interest grid (Mendelow)
- [ ] `hofstede-culture`: cultural dimensions for international business
- [ ] `okr`: objectives and key results
- [ ] `raci`: roles and responsibilities

## Entrepreneurship

- [ ] `lean-canvas`: one-page plan for startups (Maurya)
- [ ] `customer-interviews`: interviews that avoid false positives (Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test*)
- [ ] `pitch-deck-review`: structure and weak points of a pitch deck

## Sustainability

- [ ] `materiality-matrix`: ESG issues ranked by stakeholder importance and business impact
- [ ] `triple-bottom-line`: people, planet, profit (Elkington)

## Languages

- [ ] Add German, Italian or Portuguese to the terminology tables
- [ ] Translate the README into German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese…

## Examples

- [ ] A worked example for each skill, on a real and public case
