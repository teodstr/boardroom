# VRIO

Find out which of a company's resources and capabilities create a lasting advantage, and which are just the price of entry.

## When to use

- "What is X's competitive advantage?", "Is this advantage sustainable?", internal diagnosis.
- Feeds the strengths and weaknesses of `swot-tows`.

## Inputs

1. The company and its main competitors.
2. Its candidate resources (brand, patents, locations, data, talent, culture) and capabilities (processes, know-how, relationships).

## Method

1. **List 6–10 candidate resources and capabilities**, as specific as possible ("network of 1,200 franchised stores in Spain", not "distribution").
2. **Ask the four questions in order** for each one, and stop at the first "no":
   - **Valuable:** does it let the company seize an opportunity or neutralize a threat (more revenue or lower cost)? No → competitive disadvantage.
   - **Rare:** do only a few competitors have it? No → competitive parity.
   - **Inimitable:** is it costly to copy or substitute (history, causal ambiguity, social complexity, patents)? No → temporary advantage.
   - **Organized:** is the company set up to capture the value (processes, incentives, systems)? No → unexploited advantage.
3. **Conclude for each item:** disadvantage, parity, temporary advantage, unexploited advantage or sustained advantage.
4. **Synthesize:** the 1–3 sources of sustained advantage, how to protect them, and which resources to build.

## Output

```markdown
### VRIO — <company> (vs <competitors>)
| Resource / capability | V | R | I | O | Competitive implication |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| … | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | – | Temporary advantage |

### Sustained advantages
1. <Resource>: why it is hard to copy, for how long, how to protect it.

### Gaps to close
- …
```

## Quality bar

- Resources are specific and compared with named competitors.
- The questions are asked in order: an item that is not valuable stops there.
- Inimitability is argued (why is it hard to copy, and for how long?), never assumed.
- The conclusion separates the price of entry from real advantages.
- Written in the user's language, with the standard terms below.
