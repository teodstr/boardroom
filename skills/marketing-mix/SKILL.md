# Marketing mix (4P / 7P)

Make every marketing decision serve the same positioning, then turn the decisions into a plan with dates, budgets and indicators.

## When to use

- Launching a product or service, writing a marketing plan, checking whether a brand's actions are coherent.
- Start from a target and a positioning. If they are missing, run `stp-positioning` first or state them as assumptions.

## Inputs

1. The target and the positioning.
2. The offer, the competitors' mixes, the budget and the objectives.

## Method

1. **Product:** core benefit, range, features, quality level, design, packaging, brand, services and guarantees.
2. **Price:** strategy (skimming, penetration, value-based, cost-plus, competition-based), price level against competitors, price range architecture, discount policy. Check willingness to pay and margin (see `unit-economics`).
3. **Place:** channels (direct, retail, e-commerce, marketplaces, distributors), coverage (intensive, selective, exclusive), logistics and availability.
4. **Promotion:** objective by stage (awareness, consideration, purchase, loyalty), key message, media (paid, owned, earned), budget and indicators.
5. **For services, add three Ps:**
   - **People:** staff, recruiting, training, service culture.
   - **Process:** the customer journey, waiting times, service standards.
   - **Physical evidence:** premises, design, uniforms, website. These are the tangible signs of an intangible quality.
6. **Check coherence:** every P supports the same positioning. Flag contradictions, such as a premium price sold through discount channels, or a "sustainable" brand with single-use packaging.
7. **Build the roadmap:** 4–8 actions with timing, budget and indicator.

## Quality bar

- Decisions are concrete (a price, a channel, a message), not categories.
- Each P is linked to the positioning, and contradictions are flagged.
- Prices are checked against margins and competitors. No invented market data: label assumptions.
- The plan ends with a dated roadmap and indicators.
- Written in the user's language, with the standard terms below.
